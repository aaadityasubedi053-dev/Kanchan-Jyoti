Our Mission

Our Institute is a trusted educational institution located in Barhadashi-5, Ghodamara, Jhapa. Since its establishment, our mission has been to provide quality education in a caring, disciplined, and student-friendly environment, ensuring that children in our community receive educational opportunities comparable to those in urban areas.

At Kanchan Jyoti English Boarding School, we believe that every child deserves a quality education regardless of location. We strive to provide a city-level learning experience in a rural setting, preparing students with the knowledge, confidence, and values needed to succeed in the future.
